About Vorveo

Vorveo (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd., rooted in advanced digital AV expertise since 2013, specializes in DVB-T2/S2/C, Android STBs, and high-definition receivers. We operate 5 high-speed SMT lines, 10 automated assembly lines, and hold ISO9001 & ISO14001 certifications, with an annual capacity exceeding 6 million units.

Our products are exported to Europe, Southeast Asia, South America, and Africa, providing OEM/ODM solutions for global operators. Every Vorveo set-top box undergoes rigorous RF and aging tests, delivering exceptional performance even in complex signal environments.
